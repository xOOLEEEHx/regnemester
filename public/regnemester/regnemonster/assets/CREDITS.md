`tiles/grass-textured-48.png` is a cropped 96 x 96 selection from the purchased
`Modern_Exteriors_RPG_Maker_MV/A2_Floors_MV_TILESET.png` file. It contains four
48 x 48 grass variations for use in the Regnemonster map.

`library/` contains 24 renamed, otherwise unchanged transparent singles from
the purchased
`Modern_Exteriors_48x48/Modern_Exteriors_Complete_Singles_48x48` package.
The source filenames are recorded in
`src/game/content/regnemonsterObjectCatalog.json`.

`objects/japanese-house-48.png` and
`objects/japanese-house-door-open-48.png` are renamed, unchanged singles from
the purchased `Modern_Exteriors_48x48` package.

- Creator: LimeZu

- License: May be edited and used in commercial and non-commercial projects.

  Credit with a link to the creator is required.

`interiors/collector-house.png` is the purchased
`Generic_Home_1_preview_48x48.png` design.

`interiors/game-house.png` is rasterized from the user-created
`Spillhuset.tmx` Tiled map. It uses purchased Modern Interiors tiles from the
32x32 Interiors, Generic Home and TV Studio folders.

`interiors/binder-table.png` is the renamed
`Classroom_and_Library_Singles_48x48_25.png` single.
